# CourseCompass — University Academic Benefits & EduRevolution Guide

This guide details the **Lovely Professional University (LPU) EduRevolution (EduRev)** framework, its 8 academic benefit schemes, eligibility criteria, stacking rules, and official application workflows.

---

## 1. What is EduRevolution?

**EduRevolution (EduRev)** is LPU's flagship academic policy framework designed to transition university education from traditional classroom seat-time to experiential, outcome-driven learning. Under EduRevolution, **up to 50% of course credits and academic assessments** can be fulfilled through real-world achievements:
- Professional internships and industry projects.
- Hackathons, competitive programming, and technical innovation.
- Research publications in Scopus-indexed journals and patents.
- High-performing entrepreneurship and commercial revenue generation.
- Industry-recognized proctored certifications and MOOCs (NPTEL/Swayam).

---

## 2. The 8 Core Benefit Schemes

### Scheme 1: 10% Attendance Benefit
Allows eligible students to receive up to a **10% relaxation in mandatory attendance** for approved academic, competitive exam, or professional engagements.

#### Eligibility Conditions:
- **Mandatory CGPA**: Minimum **$\ge 7.5$** by the end of the term.
- **Minimum Raw Attendance**: Must maintain at least **60% attendance** without any bonus or Duty Leave.
- **Disciplinary Standing**: Clean record with no disciplinary actions.
- **Year of Study**:
  - Competitive Exams: Must be in **pre-final or final year** (for programs $> 3$ years); otherwise final-year only.
- **Approved Exams**: GATE, CAT, GRE, GMAT, UPSC CSE, State Civil Services, SSB, SSC, UGC NET, CSIR, etc.

#### Required Documents:
- Application form & Admit Card.
- Result Card / Score Card (once declared).
- Travel tickets (for exam days).

---

### Scheme 2: Duty Leave (DL) under EDU Revolution
Grants formal excused absence from scheduled class hours for participating in recognized competitions, competitive examinations, or conferences.

#### Conditions:
- Minimum **60% raw attendance** maintained.
- **Recruitment / Competitive Exams**: Excused absence for exam days + travel days (maximum 3 days DL). *Not applicable for 1st-year students.*
- **Technical Competitions / Hackathons**:
  - National or International level events.
  - Host organization must be **NIRF-ranked**, an IIT/NIT, or a prime enterprise/leading corporate (e.g., Smart India Hackathon, Microsoft Imagine Cup, RoboCon).

### Scheme 5: NPTEL / MOOCs Equivalence
Enables students to substitute regular LPU courses with certified online courses from Swayam/NPTEL.
- **CGPA Requirement**: **$\ge 7.0$** for full course waiver.
- **Proctoring**: The MOOC exam must be **in-person center proctored**.
- **Credit Transfer Cap**: Up to a maximum of **40% of total program credits** (UGC regulations).

---

### Scheme 6: Project-Based Benefits
For students working on formal industry-funded or government-sponsored research projects (DST, DRDO, MeitY) or incubation centre ventures.
- Grants project credit substitutions and attendance duty leave upon approval from the standing committee.

---

### Scheme 7: Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)
Allows experienced students holding professional certifications or verified work experience prior to term enrollment to waive introductory courses.
- Requires securing at least a **B+ grade** in the university's formal RPL evaluation examination.

---

### Scheme 8: Student-Centric Revenue Generation Model (SCRGM)
Rewards students who generate verified commercial revenue through freelance consulting, software development, or startups while studying:
- **₹25,000 – ₹50,000**: CA waivers in mapped courses.
- **₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000**: Full CA + MTE waiver.
- **> ₹1,00,000**: Full course equivalence or grade boost upon verification of GST/ITR invoices.

## 3. Stacking & Optimization Strategy

When a student holds multiple achievements in the same term, the **CourseCompass Optimizer** applies LPU's official stacking constraints:

1. **One Benefit per Course per Term**: A single enrolled course can only have **one** mapped academic benefit (e.g., you cannot apply both a Hackathon Grade Up and an Internship CA waiver to `CSE205`).
2. **Prioritize High-Credit Courses**:
   - High-impact awards (Grade Up to 'O' or Full CA+MTE) should always be mapped to **4-credit core courses** (e.g., Data Structures, Operating Systems) to yield the largest SGPA boost.
   - Lower-tier waivers (1 CA waiver) should be mapped to 2-credit or 3-credit courses.
3. **University-Wide Attendance Stacking**:
   - The **10% Attendance Benefit** applies across **all courses simultaneously** without consuming a course's single benefit slot, provided the student has $\text{CGPA} \ge 7.5$ and raw attendance $\ge 60\%$.
4. **Duty Leave Non-Exclusivity**:
   - Duty Leave (DL) for event/travel days is independent and does not restrict course-level academic benefits.

## 4. Official UMS Application Workflow

Students must submit their nominations through the official university portal:

```text
[Step 1: Select Category]
           ↓
UMS Navigation >>> Placement Services / Academic Services >>> Special Academic Benefit
           ↓
[Step 2: Choose Course]
Map your achievement to an eligible course enrolled in your current semester
           ↓
[Step 3: Upload Proofs]
Upload signed Offer Letter, Completion Certificate, Bank Statements, or Admit Cards
           ↓
[Step 4: Standing Committee Approval]
Standing Committee verifies documents and updates UMS grade / attendance records
```
